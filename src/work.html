Specialty Retail Salesperson - Plankebyen Elektriske

Whilst not being in tech, it was within the electrician field of retail. Where I was responsible for maintaining the small shop that Plankebyen Elektriske had open during the summer for three seasons, and Saturdays the rest of the year. My work consisted of ordering supplies for the store, counting and recording cash, handling customers, sales, repairing small electronic appliances, and configuring routers, where on some occasions I was sent on home visits to fix and troubleshoot routers in the area. Aside from my shop duties, I became the unofficial receptionist of the business, where I recorded contact information that would be used for customers electrician job requests, and on top of that I became the office’s unofficial IT support.
